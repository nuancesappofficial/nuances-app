# Subscription Terms Clause

Use this clause in the Nuances Terms of Service subscription section.

## Subscriptions and In-App Purchases

Nuances offers optional premium features through auto-renewing subscriptions purchased with Apple's in-app purchase system.

- Payment is charged to your Apple ID account when you confirm the purchase.
- If a free trial is offered, the subscription automatically converts to a paid subscription unless canceled before the trial ends.
- Subscriptions automatically renew unless canceled at least 24 hours before the end of the current subscription period.
- Your Apple ID account may be charged for renewal within 24 hours before the end of the current period.
- Apple shows the subscription price, trial terms, renewal timing, and effective dates before purchase or plan changes are confirmed.
- You can manage, cancel, upgrade, or downgrade your subscription in Apple subscription settings.
- Nuances may provide a Manage or cancel subscription button in the membership screen or settings screen that opens Apple subscription settings.
- Cancellation stops future renewals but does not necessarily refund the current paid period. Refund requests are handled by Apple according to Apple's policies.
- Nuances does not collect or store your payment card information.
