## Sign In

Nuances supports Sign in with Apple. If you cannot sign in, confirm that you are using the same Apple ID you used when creating your Nuances account.

## Subscriptions

Nuances Pro unlocks premium features such as AI card generation, cloud text-to-speech, pronunciation scoring, and expanded learning workflows.

Subscriptions are processed by Apple through in-app purchase. Nuances does not collect or store your credit card information.

## Restore Purchases

If you purchased Nuances Pro but premium features are not active:

1. Open Nuances.
2. Go to Profile or Settings.
3. Open the membership screen.
4. Tap Restore Purchases.
5. Make sure you are signed in with the same Apple ID used for the purchase.

## Cancel or Manage Subscription

You can manage or cancel your subscription through Apple:

1. Open the iOS Settings app.
2. Tap your Apple ID profile.
3. Tap Subscriptions.
4. Select Nuances.
5. Manage or cancel the subscription.

You can also open Apple subscription settings from the Nuances membership screen when your subscription is active.

## Change Subscription Plan

Subscription upgrades, downgrades, renewals, and cancellations are managed by Apple. If you change from one Nuances Pro plan to another, Apple will show the effective date, renewal price, and billing details before confirming the change.

You can change your subscription plan from Apple subscription settings:

1. Open the iOS Settings app.
2. Tap your Apple ID profile.
3. Tap Subscriptions.
4. Select Nuances.
5. Choose the plan you want to switch to, or cancel the subscription.

When your Nuances Pro subscription is active, the Nuances membership screen also includes a Manage or cancel subscription button that opens Apple subscription settings.

## Free Trial

If a free trial is offered, it automatically converts to a paid subscription unless canceled before the trial ends. Apple sends subscription and billing notices according to your App Store account settings.

## Account Deletion

You can delete your account inside Nuances:

1. Open Nuances.
2. Go to Settings.
3. Choose Delete Account.
4. Confirm the deletion prompt.

Deleting your account removes your Supabase auth account, profile, cards, cached items, media, review history, sync metadata, and subscription records from Nuances active systems. Apple subscription billing is managed separately by Apple, so cancel any active subscription from Apple subscription settings if needed.
