# Visual and delivery contract

## Canvas

- Apple App Store iPhone 6.9-inch portrait export
- exactly `1290 × 2796 px`
- PNG, RGB or explicitly approved RGBA
- upright phone, no dramatic perspective
- one benefit, one phone, and one enlarged highlight per panel

## Composition defaults

- Keep the upper region clean for direct text without a container.
- Begin the headline near 7–10% of canvas height; use one or two balanced lines.
- Place the subtitle about 2–3% of canvas height below the headline.
- Begin the phone near 25–29% of canvas height and keep the complete body visible.
- Keep highlight edges at least 5–7% of canvas width from outer edges.
- Allow highlights to exceed phone width while remaining safely inside the canvas.

Use these as starting ranges, not universal constants. The approved visual reference is authoritative.

## Style

Prefer a light ice-gray panorama, brand-colored flowing ribbons, rounded benefit-led typography, high UI/background contrast, restrained shadows, and soft depth already supported by the app's design.

Reject large caption containers, excessive glow, heavy feathering, tilted or stretched phones, regenerated UI, multiple competing highlights, hard panorama seams, mirrored waves, blur patches, and decorative elements that obscure the app.

## Panorama continuity

At every boundary, preserve ribbon position, thickness, color, stacking order, direction, and curvature tangent. Inspect the master panorama and no-gap contact sheet at full resolution. Do not hide a seam under the phone as a substitute for continuity.

## Caption fidelity

Type text after raster composition. Use a real rounded sans-serif supporting the locale. Lock headline color, weight, maximum width, and reference block position. Fit by measured glyph bounds. Compare resulting pixel height and vertical center to the reference; point-size equality is insufficient.

Avoid one-character line breaks and unexpectedly tiny two-line translations. When a reference uses one line but a localization uses two, preserve visual prominence by allocating a two-line title-height budget.

## Highlight fidelity

The enlarged content must already exist in raw or approved reference pixels. Uniformly scale it. Keep complete content above and below; rounded masks must not clip glyph ascenders, descenders, IPA slashes, scores, borders, or glow.

Use a soft multi-alpha shadow rather than a second opaque pill. Preserve selected-state colors from the reference. Any glyph-removal repair must be visually continuous with surrounding glass at all four edges.

## Per-panel checklist

1. Exact canvas, format, and filename.
2. Correct raw state and locale.
3. Upright proportional complete phone.
4. Exact approved copy, sharp typed text, and reference-scale caption.
5. Exactly one visually located, unclipped, authentic highlight.
6. Correct invariant/localized content policy.
7. Highlight above all background elements with clean edges.
8. Continuous panorama on both sides.
9. No fuzzy text, hallucinated UI, distorted crop, gray padding, or repair patch.
10. Full-resolution and contact-sheet review completed.
