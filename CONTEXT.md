# Nuances App

A contextual English learning and flashcard application that helps users master subtle language nuances, formal/informal scales, and native collocations.

## Language

### Card & Content

**Card Subject**:
The specific, contextual meaning and usage of a word that a flashcard is built around for primary learning and SRS review.
_Avoid_: dictionary entry, omnibus word card

**Supplementary Senses**:
Other major definitions and parts of speech of the word outside of the card's specific contextual meaning, generated by AI and displayed on the card's back face in a dedicated section for quick reference.
_Avoid_: dictionary list, full lexicon dump

**Nuance Metrics**:
Visual dual-axis indicators (formality and intensity) representing the tone and pragmatic depth of a word within its specific sentence context.
_Avoid_: sliders, levers

**Section Badge**:
A unified subtle tinted pill (`bg-slate-200/60` / `bg-slate-800`, `text-slate-500`, `text-xs`, `font-semibold`, rounded-full) applied to section titles on both card front (Context) and card back (Collocations, Synonyms, Antonyms, Cultural Background, Supplementary Senses).
_Avoid_: plain section text, floating header

### Deck & Organization

**Batch Selection Mode**:
A temporary list interaction state where individual card three-dot menus convert to checkable circles, and a floating bottom dock handles batch folder moving or deletion.
_Avoid_: edit mode, card manager mode

**Album**:
A folder-like collection of cards organized by topic, scenario, or learning track.
_Avoid_: category, directory, playlist
